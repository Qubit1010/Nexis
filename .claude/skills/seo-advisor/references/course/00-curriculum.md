# The 2026 SEO Playbook - Curriculum & Learning Roadmap

*Four tiers. Forty-two sections. One site taken from invisible to ranked.*

**Bottom line:** This is a build-along course, not a reading list. Every section ends with
a lab you actually run, and one real site (nexus-point.co) carries through all four tiers
as a capstone. If you do the labs, you finish with a ranked, measured, AI-visible site and
a service you can sell. If you only read, you finish with notes.

---

## How to use this

- **One section at a time.** Say "next section" or "section 14" and it gets written from
  the corpus, in full, with its lab.
- **Do the lab before moving on.** The capstone compounds. Skipping Tier 1 measurement
  means Tier 4 has nothing to measure against.
- **Order matters inside a tier, less so between tiers 3 and 4.** If a client need forces
  you into AI search early, jump to Section 36 and come back.
- **Every claim in every section is tagged.** `[confirmed]` traces to Google documentation
  or peer-reviewed work. `[practitioner]` rests on a vendor blog or a case study. The
  corpus is 15 confirmed sources against 269 practitioner ones, which is the honest shape
  of this field. Treat the two differently.

## Prerequisites

You need almost nothing to start. Specifically:

- **Required:** a website you control (nexus-point.co), a Google account, and a browser.
- **Free tools, set up in Section 10:** Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, PageSpeed
  Insights. All free, no card.
- **Free tiers used later:** a crawler (Screaming Frog is free to 500 URLs) in Tier 3, and
  a keyword tool in Tier 1. Section 42 covers whether paid tools are worth it. You can
  complete every lab without paying for anything.
- **Not required:** coding. Sections 26 and 28 touch rendering and performance, and both
  give the non-developer version plus the fix to hand a developer.

## What this course deliberately does not cover

Being honest about scope beats pretending completeness.

- **Writing the actual article.** Handed off to the `blog-writer` skill, which owns
  article-level SEO on its own cited corpus.
- **Running a full audit as a deliverable.** Handed off to `website-audit-system`.
- **Paid search.** SEM and Google Ads are a different discipline.
- **Ecommerce SEO at depth.** Faceted navigation and product schema get covered in
  Sections 25 and 30, but marketplace and catalog strategy does not.
- **Programmatic SEO at scale.** Touched in Section 25, but the full playbook lives in
  `marketing-skills/programmatic-seo` (uncited, treat accordingly).
