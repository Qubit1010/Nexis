# Prompt craft: stills, motion, and what to reject

## Still prompts (Nano Banana Pro, 2K, 9:16)

Write each still like a cinematographer's frame description, in this order:

1. **Shot type and lens feel:** "cinematic close-up", "macro slow-motion photograph",
   "top-down aerial from very high altitude", "wide vertical shot".
2. **Subject and action**, with characters as `<<<element_id>>>` and a precise, physical
   description of what their hands are doing.
3. **Setting with culturally specific props:** "sadu-pattern floor cushions", "brass dallah",
   "handleless finjan", "carved rectangular wooden shutters", "antique mandoos chest with brass studs".
4. **Light:** one named source and time: "low amber evening sunbeam through the shutters",
   "single brass oil lantern", "shafts of sunlight piercing turquoise water".
5. **Look:** "shallow depth of field, 65mm film look, ultra photoreal".
6. **Negatives that target known failure modes**, not a generic list: "no text", "no modern
   boat", "no prayer beads, no tassel", "no smoke, no vapor", "no arched windows", "not
   resembling any real person".

Hands: say "anatomically correct with five fingers each" whenever hands are the subject.
Science/micro scenes: describe them like deep-space astrophotography (luminous, volumetric,
bokeh, a sense of scale). That is what makes them feel awe-inspiring rather than clip-art.
Use the brand hex colours in those scenes (e.g. "deep navy #1C2440, sky blue #7CB0DF").

## Motion prompts (Kling 3.0 Pro from a start still)

Describe only the motion, slow and physical, then the camera: "the grandfather gently lowers
the necklace into the boy's hands … slow push-in, smooth continuous shot, no cuts". One action
per clip. Add a negative when the still invites a known drift: "he stays in the water and does
not stand up", "no creatures", "no smoke".

For to-camera speaking shots add "minimal mouth movement": the voice is added later and never
lip-syncs (see story-craft.md).

## Rejection checklist (real failures from CURE, all caught at the still stage)

Look at every still and every clip strip for:

| Seen | Why it failed | Fix that worked |
|---|---|---|
| A modern motorboat above the diver | anachronism | name the "traditional wooden dhow hull with a high curved prow" |
| A folded protein that looked like cursive letters | reads as text/writing | "compact globular fold with helices and sheets, does not resemble letters" |
| A diver wading chest-deep instead of surfacing | wrong story beat | "treading water, only head and shoulders above the surface" |
| An exhaled breath rendered as a dark puff | reads as smoking (health-ad red line) | "no smoke, no vapor, no mist cloud" |
| Jellyfish inside the body world | breaks the metaphor | "no creatures" |
| A loose pearl strand between two hands; a strand across an open palm | reads as a misbaha | make it jewellery: gold clasp/pendant, held up by the clasp, or worn |
| Both palms open and raised | reads as du'a | "holding it up in one hand, other hand on her lap" |
| A handled teacup and saucer | not gahwa culture | "tiny handleless white finjan held between the fingertips" |
| The elder pouring for the young | hospitality order inverted | the younger person stands and pours for the elder |
| A fort-like wall or arched mosque-style windows behind a family | royal or religious associations | "plain cream walls", "rectangular carved wooden shutters", "no arched windows" |
| Gold calligraphy forming letter-like shapes | may read as a real word | flag for a native reader, or prompt "abstract strokes, no readable words" |

Reroll a still at most three times. If the third is still borderline, use the best one and flag
it in the review notes for the human cultural reviewer rather than spending more.
