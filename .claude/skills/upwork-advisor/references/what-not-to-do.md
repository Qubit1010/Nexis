# What Not To Do — retired Upwork tactics

Advice that was true, is now wrong or actively harmful, or was never sourced. Check here before
repeating something that "everyone knows" about Upwork. Every entry cites the section of
`research-synthesis.md` that retires it.

---

## Volume and bidding

| Retired tactic | Why it's wrong now |
|---|---|
| "Send more proposals, it's a numbers game" | If win rate is low, volume and boosting **accelerate losses** [Q4]. Top performers hit 30-50% win rates by applying to *far fewer* jobs [Q5] |
| "Automate proposal sending to scale" | AI-generated proposals underperform and are **actively filtered** by platform and clients [Q5] |
| "Bid on everything to build history" | Unverified-payment and sub-30%-hire-rate clients are negative EV [Q3]. Fit threshold ~7.5/10 [Q8] |
| "Always boost to stay competitive" | Only pays above **$1,000** job value with directly relevant proof [Q4] |
| "Apply to older jobs, less competition" | 24-48h+ old or 20+ proposals = closed window [Q3][Q5] |

## Profile

| Retired tactic | Why it's wrong now |
|---|---|
| "Add every skill tag you have, more keywords = more visibility" | **Irrelevant tags dilute relevance and cause de-ranking.** Exactly 15, niche-matched [Q1][Q2] |
| "Position as a generalist so you qualify for more jobs" | Algorithm favors specialists [Q2]; specialists earn **~35% more** [Q6] |
| "Open your overview with your background and experience" | First two lines are a **search preview**. Self-centered "I" openers underperform [Q1][Q5] |
| "Portfolio is just screenshots of past work" | Each piece needs problem + solution + **measurable outcome** [Q1] |
| "Ignore invites that don't fit" | Invite-to-hire ratio is now a signal. **Decline explicitly with a reason** — it trains the matcher [Q2] |

## Pricing

| Retired tactic | Why it's wrong now |
|---|---|
| "Start cheap to build reviews, raise later" | Contradicted across Q6/Q8: specialists command premiums; price is not the lever. The undercharging tell is being consistently booked out [Q6] |
| "Discount to win the first project with a client" | **Cut scope, never the rate** [Q6], corroborated independently by `upwork-reply-drafter` Q2 |
| "Match the posted budget" | Budget is an anchor, not a ceiling. Propose the real price with a one-line justification [Q3] |
| "Quote your take-home rate" | Upwork's fee comes off the top — see `upwork-reply-drafter`'s mechanics file |

## Platform beliefs

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| "Upwork is dead / oversaturated" | **Not supported.** It shifted to favoring specialists and precision; commodity work is what's eroding [Q8]. Diagnose the real cause (see `strategy-2026.md`) |
| "AI is destroying freelance demand" | Half-true: it removed low-complexity work *and* raised the premium on complex work. Complex-AI freelancers earn **+34%** [Q8] |
| "JSS is a snapshot you can fix quickly" | It's **sustained** — 90% across 13 of 16 weeks for Top Rated [Q7]. A dip costs weeks |
| "Specialized profiles are the way to cover multiple services" | **Unverified.** One source says they were phased out May 2026 [Q1]. Check the live account before advising either way |

## Things this skill must never do

- **Write a proposal or a client reply.** Route to `upwork-proposal-generator` / `upwork-reply-drafter`.
- **Route to `projects/upwork-job-scout`.** It's inactive (no API access). Use the manual rubric.
- **Invent a benchmark.** If it's not in `upwork-scoreboard.md` or the synthesis, say so — the
  "Known gaps" list at the bottom of the synthesis is the honest answer to several common questions.
- **Advise closing a contract to trigger a review.** The client closes it [`upwork-reply-drafter` Q4/Q5].
- **Quote pre-2025 platform mechanics** — connect pricing, fee tiers, and badge rules all moved.
