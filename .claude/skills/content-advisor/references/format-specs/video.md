# Video — long-form video, shorts, reels, TikToks

Four formats, one mechanic that separates them from everything else: **time is controlled by
the video, not the reader.** The viewer cannot scan ahead. That makes the opening seconds and
the retention curve the whole game.

Tiers per `00-index.md`. **Specifications are `[P*]` and must be re-verified on the day used.**

---

## The evidence that applies to all four

**Engagement drops sharply after about six minutes.** Across **6.9 million viewing sessions**,
viewers watched at most roughly six minutes regardless of total length `[C]`. This is
educational-video research, not marketing data, and it should be attributed that way - it
transfers well to teaching content and less well to a brand film.

**Segmenting reduces cognitive load and improves retention** `[C]` [s41][s183]. Explicit
segments with their own payoffs beat an undifferentiated run.

**Showing a face improves retention and directs visual attention** `[C]` [s190][s24].
Screencast and step-by-step demonstration beat slides over the same material; recorded lecture
capture performs worst `[C]` [s24].

**Completion rate is duration-biased by construction.** Shorter items achieve higher completion
mechanically, longer ones accumulate more watch time, and looping distorts both `[C]` [s31].
Feeds also carry strong position bias `[C]` [s31]. **Never compare completion across different
lengths.**

**"85% of video is watched without sound" is not substantiated** anywhere in this corpus. Caption
anyway: captions have measured comprehension and retention effects `[C]` [s41] and accessibility
requirements are normative `[C]` [s62]. The reason is not the statistic.

## Long-form video (YouTube)

**What it is for.** Depth, search visibility, and the one format where a stranger will give you
ten minutes.

**Structure.** Cold open on the payoff or the tension, before any branding. The promise - what
they will be able to do by the end. Chapters, each a self-contained unit with its own small
payoff. Retention beats where the pattern changes. The payoff. One end-screen action. `[K]`
[s386][s399][s401]

**The hook.** First 30 seconds. Craft sources converge on opening with the result or the
conflict and cutting the introduction entirely. `[K]` [s388][s400]

**Length and pacing.** Set by the content, but segment it - the six-minute finding is about
attention within a stretch, not a hard ceiling on total length. Chapters are how a long video
survives it.

**2026 optimization.** Title and thumbnail are designed together, not sequentially `[K]`
[s403]. Description first two lines carry the search-visible summary. Creators re-cut against
their own retention graphs - that is the actual feedback loop `[K]` [s399].

**Distribution.** Search plus subscriber base on day one, then clips into short-form.

**Fails when** it opens with a channel intro, or when it is a webinar recording nobody re-cut.

**Who executes it.** `content-production` scripts it. No in-house renderer for long-form.

## Shorts, Reels and TikToks

Treated together because the mechanics are near-identical and the differences are conventions,
not findings. **Where they genuinely differ is specification, which is `[P*]`.**

**What it is for.** Reach beyond the existing audience. These are feed-ranked, not
follower-ranked, which is why they are the discovery format.

**Structure.** Hook in the first seconds, spoken and on-screen. Setup with the stake in one
line. Body in beats, one idea each. Payoff. Loop or CTA. `[K]` [s307][s323][s330]

**The hook.** First 3 seconds, and it has to work on mute. Craft consensus is to open on the
result, the conflict or a visual anomaly. `[K]` [s316][s335][s340]

**Length and pacing.** Shorter is not automatically better - it wins completion mechanically
`[C]` [s31], which is a measurement artefact rather than a quality signal. Set length by the
number of beats the idea needs.

**2026 optimization.** On-screen text per beat. Loop construction so the end runs into the
beginning. Sound is a discovery mechanic, not a delivery one - assume mute for comprehension and
use audio for reach. `[K]` [s336][s342][s344]

**Distribution.** Native upload per platform. **Do not cross-post one file** - it is the same
argument as not reusing one text across platforms, and the specs differ.

**Fails when** the first three seconds are branding, when the idea needs more beats than the
length allows, or when performance is judged on completion rate.

**Who executes it.**

| | Owner |
|---|---|
| 9:16 static frames (cover, content, CTA) | `shorts-creator` |
| Rendered, voiced motion-graphics reel | `reel-creator`, `hyperframes-reel` |
| TikTok and short-video scripts | `content-production` |
| Clips from a podcast transcript | `podcast-repurposer` |
