## The 2026 reality: Interest Graph, not Social Graph

LinkedIn now runs on "360Brew," a 150B-parameter AI that reads the **semantic meaning** of your posts and matches them to people by interest, not by who you're connected to [s185, s186]. Consequences:
- A good post from a small account can reach widely (niche authority beats follower count).
- Generic, GPT-flavored, "bro-etry" copy gets throttled - the AI detects it.
- Overall organic reach fell hard after the shift (impressions -63-66%, views -50%, engagement -25%). The bar is higher; substance wins.

**The 3-stage distribution you're playing against** [s186]:
1. **Golden Hour (0-60 min):** post is shown to **2-5% of your network**. It needs **5-10% engagement** in that window to advance. Below 2% and distribution dies (kills ~87% of posts). Only 5% of weak starters ever recover.
2. **Expanded (1-6 hrs):** survivors go to 10-20% of network + 2nd-degree.
3. **Dwell time is the #1 signal:** 0-3s dwell = 1.2% ER; **61s+ dwell = 15.6% ER (13x)**. Write posts people *stop and read*, not skim-and-like.

## Content formats, ranked by 2026 performance

| Format | Engagement rate | Play |
|--------|----------------|------|
| **Document/PDF carousel** | **6.6-7.0%** (highest) | 5-15 slides; teach one idea visually. +278% vs video [s146] |
| **Native vertical video** | 4.7-5.6% | 30-90s, hook in first 1-2s, 9:16 (=+71% impressions) [s185] |
| **Text post** | 2.0-4.5% (expert 6-8%) | 150-300 words / 800-1000 chars to earn the "see more" click [s186] |
| **Newsletter** | **40-60% open** | Bypasses the feed (email+push). Start one for compounding reach [s154] |

**Rules that move reach:**
- **No external links in the post body** (-60-68% reach). Don't put the link in the first comment either - that's penalized now in 2026. Put the link in the post only when the post IS a resource directory (3+ links can lift +236%) [s185]. Otherwise: "comment X and I'll send it" or link from profile.
- **Personal profile >> company page** (8x engagement, 5.6x reach). Lead with Aleem's face/voice, not the NexusPoint page [s85].
- **1-2 hashtags max, or none** - the AI reads context; 3-5 hashtags actually cut reach 29% [s186].
- **Comments are gold:** a thoughtful 10+ word comment carries 5-7x the weight of a like; threaded back-and-forth = 5.2x amplification. Reply to every comment in the first 60 min.

## Posting cadence & timing

- **2-5 posts/week** (3-5 optimal, 2 is the floor of the band - not below it). More isn't better; daily posting with weak engagement throttles you [s146, s43].
- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday.
- **Windows (local time):** 7-9am, 8-10am, 12-1pm, 2-4pm. Avoid Monday AM, Friday PM, weekends (-30-60%) [s222].
- **Engage before you post:** comment on 5-10 niche posts in the 15 min before publishing to prime distribution.

## Growing the account (added 2026-07-27)

`[Ln]` citations below are **net-new sources from the 2026-07-27 live pass, not the locked 234-source corpus** - resolve in `research-synthesis.md` -> Live Query Additions [2026-07-27].

### Is follower growth even the goal?

`channel-benchmarks.md` lists follower counts as a vanity metric to ignore [s206]. That is right **as a KPI** and wrong as a constraint. Below roughly 5K followers the base itself caps absolute reach, so audience size is a real bottleneck even when efficiency is excellent. Hold both: measure **profile views -> conversations**, and treat follower count as a leading indicator of the reach ceiling, never as the scoreboard. Do not set a follower target.

And say the timeline honestly: personal-brand ramp to compounding inbound is **12-24 months** [s110]. LinkedIn organic is not the channel that closes this quarter's direct clients. Outreach is.

### Aleem's baseline (2026-07-27)

1,798 followers. Past 7 days: 1,465 impressions, 184 profile views, 25 search appearances.

| Ratio | Value | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions/post (~2 posts) | **~733** | **1.5x** the 1-5K bracket median of 479 [L1]; 0.85x the all-profile median of 860 [L3] |
| Reach rate per post | **40.7%** of followers | Top quartile - the threshold is 20%+ [L4] |
| Profile-view rate | **12.6%** of impressions | Strong; people who see him go look |
| Search share of profile views | **≤13.6%** | Profile SEO has a low ceiling here |

**Golden Hour verdict: his posts are not dying there.** Stage 2 tops out at 10-20% of network = 180-360 people at his size. At ~733 impressions/post he clears it roughly 2x. **Skip all hook/timing/first-60-minutes advice for this account** - it is not the constraint, and it is exactly what generic LinkedIn advice leads with.

**Diagnosis: reach efficiency is top-quartile, absolute volume is median. A small-base problem, not a quality problem.** Posting is capped at 2x/week by his own Q4 decision, so the only lever that adds surface area without adding posts is commenting.

Directional, not exact: LinkedIn's weekly impressions can include older posts, profile views include non-feed sources, and the per-post figure assumes exactly 2 posts. **Two numbers to fetch before this section is complete** - net new followers for the period (Analytics -> Followers), and per-post ER (reactions+comments+reposts ÷ impressions; <2% kills distribution, 5-10% advances it).

### Operating at 2 posts/week

2/week is the **floor of the documented 2-5 band, not below it** [s43]. Both slots go to the highest-ER format he can sustain - document carousel at 6.6-7% - not to filler. The missing 1-3 posts are compensated by commenting, not by lowering the bar per post.

### Growth levers, ranked by return per unit of effort

1. **Commenting - the one habit to add.** Comments borrow distribution from audiences that already exist. Top creators comment **~150/week**; the platform hard cap is **90/day** [L6]. Tier the targets: large creators for visibility, mid-sized for relationships, peers for community. LinkedIn now shows **impression counts on your own comments** (self-visible), so this is finally measurable - track it like a post [L7]. Substantive only: a 10+ word comment carries 5-7x the weight of a like [s186]. **Never engagement pods** - 97% detection, permanent reach cut [s186].
   - **Execution lives in the `linkedin-commenter` skill** (`.claude/skills/linkedin-commenter/`), built 2026-07-31. It pulls recent posts from the profiles in `docs/linkedin-profiles-posts.txt`, ranks them, writes a dated file, and drafts a comment per post. **Commenting stays manual** - the skill only decides where to look and what to say. Runs on an Apify actor with no LinkedIn cookie or session, so the account is never touched. ~$0.16/run at 27 profiles. Strategy stays here; run the skill for the daily habit.
   - **One finding from the first live run worth carrying back into strategy:** a hard "skip anything past 40 comments" rule sounds right and is unusable on a list of large creators. Of 9 posts sampled, comment counts ran 28 to 650 and only one cleared 40. Saturation also arrives fast (28 comments at 4.9h, 76 by 10.4h), so on big accounts the early window is roughly the first 2-3 hours or not at all. The skill now prices crowding into a score instead of cutting on it. **The strategic read: a target list of only large creators structurally cannot support the "be early" play**, which is the case for weighting toward mid-sized and peer creators rather than a tactic to tune.
2. **Creator Mode / Follow button - one-time, ~5 minutes.** Makes Follow the primary profile CTA so comment-driven visits convert to followers instead of bouncing. Relocated in 2026 to Settings -> Privacy -> Visibility -> Followers [L11]. Also what unlocks newsletters.
3. **Newsletter - feed-independent compounding.** 40-60% open rate, triggers email + push [s154]. Repurpose an existing post; do not add net-new writing to a 2x/week schedule.
4. **Collabs / podcast guesting - no 2026 evidence found.** The live pass returned no quantified outcomes for either. Listed so it isn't mistaken for an untried idea; not recommended on evidence.

### Another way in 2026: Thought Leader Ads

A company page sponsors a **personal** post. The cheapest clicks on LinkedIn: median CPC **$2.29** (range $0.50-2.00, avg $1.25) versus single-image **$13.23** and video **$15.61**, and LinkedIn reports up to **2x the CTR** of standard Sponsored Content [L8, L9].

- **Platform minimum: $10/day ($100 lifetime).** Practitioner programs report starting at **$1,500-2,000/mo** [L8, L9]. Be honest about that gap - a $300/mo test is possible but sits well below where operators report programs working. Treat sub-$300/mo as a learning experiment, not a channel.
- **Rule: only boost a post that already beat organic median.** For this account that means >733 impressions. Never boost a cold post - TLA amplifies distribution, it does not fix creative.
- **Gap:** no CPM data in any source found, so impressions-per-dollar cannot be computed. Don't estimate it.

### Decision rules

- **Links - one rule, no workaround.** Never in the body (-60-68% reach) and never in the first comment (penalized). The wait-30-60-min version is only ever "least bad," not safe. Use **"comment X and I'll send it"** or link from the profile. Exception: a genuine directory post with 3+ useful links (+236%) [s185].
- **SSI:** appears in this corpus only as a connection-volume gate (>65 unlocks higher weekly request caps). It is **not** documented anywhere here as a feed-distribution signal. Don't optimize it for reach.
- **Cadence:** 2-5/week is the band, 3-5 is the optimum. Two is a floor, not a failure.

## Outreach benchmarks (strategy here, copy in sales-playbook)

Use these to set expectations and pace volume. The actual connection note + DM sequence = **sales-playbook**.

- **Connection acceptance:** platform avg 28.5-30%. Generic mass: 12-18%. **Personalized + pre-warmed by engaging their content first: 55-70%** [s156, s149]. Engagement-before-request is the single biggest lever.
- **Safe volume:** 80-100 requests/week manually (20-25/wk if using cloud automation). Drop below 20% acceptance and LinkedIn caps you to 20-50/wk [s156]. Protect the account.
- **Reply rates:** connection-note reply is down to 2.2-3.0% (don't pitch in the note). Post-acceptance DM averages 10.4%; good is 10-25%, elite multi-touch 30-50% [s156, s157].
- **InMail (paid):** 57.5% open, 10-25% reply [s148].
- **Omnichannel:** pairing LinkedIn with email + phone lifts purchase rate +287% vs email alone [s150]. Coordinate with the cold-outreach and email pipelines.

**Sequencing principle:** warm before you ask. Engage their content -> personalized connection (no pitch) -> after accept, open with a genuine question or value, not a pitch. Full archetypes and the 4-touch sequence: sales-playbook.
