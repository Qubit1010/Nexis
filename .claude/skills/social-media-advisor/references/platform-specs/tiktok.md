# TikTok

**Corpus depth: the thinnest of the five "deep" platforms, 16 sources, 5 first-party.** Enough
to answer how the For You feed is described, not enough for confident tactical detail. Say so.
All `[P*]` retrieved **2026-08-21**.

---

## 1. What it is for

Reaching people who do not know the brand exists. TikTok is the clearest case in this corpus of
a **pure recommendation platform**: distribution is decided by the feed, not by an audience the
account already owns.

Not for: B2B with a long committee sale, brands that cannot produce native-feeling video, or
anyone whose compliance posture cannot survive a fast-moving comment section.

## 2. How it ranks — `[P*]`

TikTok documents the For You feed as ranking "based on a combination of factors" driven by
"user preferences as expressed through interactions with the app, like posting a comment or
following an account", with signals indicating "the content you like as well as the content
you'd prefer to skip" `[P*]` [s290][s291][s310].

Two things to take from this and no more:

- **The feed is per-user.** "On TikTok, the For You feed reflects preferences unique to each
  user" `[P*]` [s310]. There is no single ranking to optimise against, which is also why
  best-time and universal-tactic advice fails hardest here.
- **Negative signals count.** Skips are explicitly named as input `[P*]` [s290]. Optimising only
  for positive engagement misses half the documented mechanism.

TikTok also ships a viewer-facing "why am I seeing this" feature `[P*]` [s313], and documents
safety and community updates affecting distribution `[P*]` [s316].

**What this corpus does NOT support:** watch-time percentage thresholds, completion-rate
targets, sound and trend participation effects, or TikTok SEO tactics. All circulate widely;
none is first-party or confirmed here. If asked, say the corpus is thin and route to a live
check.

## 3. What changed

**Genuinely weak coverage.** The 2026 platform changes the craft pass surfaced are practitioner
reporting, and regulatory and ownership developments are outside this corpus entirely. Treat any

## 4. Formats that win

Native, vertical, sound-on video. Beyond that, `content-advisor`'s `format-specs/video.md` owns
short-form craft on a 560-source corpus and this skill does not restate it.

## 5. Cadence

No first-party statement, and the practitioner numbers here are thinner than on other platforms.
Convention only, flagged as such.

## 6. Growth mechanics

- **Follower count matters less here than anywhere else in this corpus**, because distribution
  is recommendation-first `[P*]` [s310]. Consistent with `[C]` [s55], that followers do not
  determine whether content travels.
- **Design against the skip**, not only for the like `[P*]` [s290].
- Cold-start is genuinely more achievable here than on LinkedIn. That is the honest case for
  TikTok when a client has no audience at all.

## 7. Fails when

- The buyer is a B2B committee.
- Production cannot be native. Repurposed landscape video with a logo reads as an ad.
- The brand needs message control.
- **Someone wants precise tactical thresholds.** The corpus does not have them; do not invent
  them from a neighbouring platform.
