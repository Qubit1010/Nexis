# NexusPoint folded-N variations

Created: 2026-10-02

Brief: Three closely related creative variations of the selected folded N with one blue centre point.

Rendered with the built-in imagegen tool. All three prompts use reference-folded-n.png as the reference image. Existing logo assets remain preserved.

## Recommendation

Centre Twist adds the strongest new character while preserving the recognizable N. Its opposing inner hooks direct attention around the shared blue point. Architectural Fold is sharper and more precise; Continuous Ribbon is softer and more fluid.

## Validation and production status

All previews were visually inspected for correct NexusPoint spelling, recognizable folded N silhouettes, a single blue centre circle, the existing palette and horizontal layout. The new constructions are visibly different: sharp fold seams, rounded fold seams and opposing central hooks. These are raster concept previews. Exact colour verification, SVG reconstruction, and monochrome and small-size checks remain production steps.
