# NexusPoint Centre Twist variations

Created: 2026-10-02

Brief: More variations of the selected Centre Twist N, retaining two opposing folded white paths, one central blue point and the horizontal NexusPoint wordmark.

The three final concepts were generated fresh from text with the built-in imagegen tool. The existing selected Centre Twist file remains preserved in ../2026-10-02-folded-n/nexuspoint-centre-twist-v1.png.

## Recommendation

Wrapped Nexus gives the central point the strongest framing and is the recommended refinement in this set. Faceted Twist uses crisper outer folds and diagonal ribbon planes. Spiral Bridge gives the inner paths a larger, more fluid sweep.

## Validation and production status

The previews were visually checked for the correct NexusPoint spelling, a recognizable N silhouette, one blue centre circle, horizontal arrangement and existing palette. The final variants are visibly different in their central wrap, fold geometry and ribbon sweep. Faceted Twist retains a rounded central counter despite the prompt requesting a diamond-shaped aperture.

These are raster concept previews. SVG reconstruction, exact colour verification, and monochrome and small-size checks remain production steps.
