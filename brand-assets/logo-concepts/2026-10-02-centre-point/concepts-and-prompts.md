# NexusPoint centre-point logo concepts

Created: 2026-10-02

Brief: Creative variations of the original N-and-point concept, with paths and optical geometry guiding attention toward the central point.

Rendered with the built-in imagegen tool. Each final prompt uses reference-n-and-point.png as its reference image. Existing logo assets remain preserved.

## Recommendation

Ambiguous Fold N is the strongest fit for the existing NexusPoint identity. Centripetal N is the cleanest refinement. Impossible Nexus is the strongest exploration of the requested paradox, using a Penrose-inspired triangular loop and restrained neutral facets to frame a blue centre point.

The fold in the N is an ambiguous front/back reading. The triangular design is an optical-illusion exploration. Attention returning to the point is a design intention, rather than a tested perceptual guarantee.

## Validation and production status

The final previews were visually checked for the exact NexusPoint spelling, horizontal layout, recognizable N forms in options 1 and 2, a triangular optical-loop concept in option 3, and a single blue centre circle in each. Option 3 uses additional neutral face colours from the existing agency palette: #CBD5E1 and #94A3B8.

These are raster concept previews. SVG reconstruction, exact colour verification, and monochrome and small-size checks remain production steps.

Render crisp flat vector-style artwork with broad shapes, smooth uniform white surfaces and ground-coloured geometric cuts defining optical relationships. Retain the clear uppercase N silhouette and its two substantial upright stems. Each upright turns into a descending diagonal arm aimed directly at the central blue point. Give the diagonal arms a slight inward taper and concave inner terminals that closely embrace the circular point, with a consistent narrow navy clearance around it. Use subtly curved inward contours along the diagonal while the outer stems remain strong and architectural. All inner contours should visually funnel toward the centre. Make the N's outer terminals precise oblique cuts aligned with its diagonal direction. Keep the point substantial enough to dominate the centre but contained within the N's proportions. This is a compact emblem of two paths drawn into one shared nexus, with an unmistakable N at first glance.

## 3. Impossible Nexus

A Penrose-inspired triangular loop frames one central nexus point.

Preview: nexuspoint-impossible-nexus-v1.png

```text
Design a NexusPoint logo variation centred on a genuine Penrose impossible triangle, the impossible tribar optical illusion. Use the attached image as the reference for the exact white wordmark "NexusPoint", its capital N and P, confident geometric sans-serif typography, horizontal arrangement and navy ground (#02040A).

Build the left emblem as one continuous impossible triangular object made of three thick rectangular beams. The three corner joins must appear locally solid and geometrically plausible while creating a cyclically contradictory spatial relationship: each beam appears to pass in front of the next around the triangle, so the complete object could only exist as an optical drawing. Form the classic closed Penrose tribar with exactly three connected beams, three seamlessly closed corners and one generous triangular hole in the middle. Use broad parallel beam edges and a compact balanced triangle silhouette.

Make the impossible geometry legible with precisely three flat neutral face colours: pure white (#FFFFFF) for the main beam faces, pale cool grey (#CBD5E1) for the inward faces, and restrained slate-grey (#94A3B8) for the return faces. Keep the treatment clean and diagrammatic with crisp geometric edges and uniform flat fills. Place exactly one solid pale-blue (#A6DAFF) circle at the geometric centre of the triangular aperture, visually floating in its uniform navy interior with generous clear space on every side. This blue point is the sole coloured accent and the emblem's focal anchor. The inward beam faces frame the point, bringing every corner's perspective back to the same centre.

Present one complete horizontal logo with the compact impossible triangle on the left and the exact wordmark "NexusPoint" on the right, optically centre-aligned with comfortable separation. Match the white wordmark style of the reference. Use a symbol approximately one and a half times the capital-letter height. Compose a wide 16:9 landscape image containing the single logo on a plain navy ground with generous clear space. Render as carefully constructed flat vector-style optical art.
```
