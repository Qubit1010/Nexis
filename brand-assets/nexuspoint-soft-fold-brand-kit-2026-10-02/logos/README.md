# Soft Fold logo files

Aleem approved 02-soft-fold-symbol.png on 2 October 2026. It is preserved unchanged at reference/approved-soft-fold-original.png.

Use the primary horizontal logo for general branding. Stacked lockups suit narrow layouts. Symbols suit profiles and compact markers. Transparent white artwork needs a dark ground; transparent navy artwork needs a light ground. Mono-black is truly black single-ink artwork.

All 28 SVGs contain native paths and circles, with no embedded raster or font dependency. The existing wordmark outline group is reused without alteration. The SVG production master is nexuspoint-outline-master.svg.

The normalized symbol is 271.811 by 284 units. Its centre-dot diameter is 73.742 units, approximately 26% of symbol height. Leave at least that diameter around the visible artwork. The colour dot uses exactly #02A1E1. Do not retype the wordmark or substitute Conthrax for it.

Recommended digital minimum: full lockup 240 px wide; symbol 32 px, preferably 48 px. At 16 px, the favicon shows the silhouette and centre but loses the curl detail. Print starting points: lockup 45 mm wide, symbol 10 mm tall. Inspect the actual output.

Method: native reconstruction of the selected generated PNG using adaptive cubic Bezier fitting with 0.65 source-pixel tolerance. The colour centre is an independent native circle. Symbol white-shape intersection-over-union is 99.39%; 95% of reconstructed edges are within one source pixel. This is a faithful reconstruction, not recovered designer source.

geometry-and-method.json records source bounds and geometry. validation-report.json records measured fidelity, exact-blue verification and the unchanged wordmark check. Rebuild with scripts/build-soft-fold-logo.py and scripts/build-logo-assets.cjs.
