The Process

We don't guess. We engineer.

The exact path from the repeating work to a system your team runs on its own. Five steps, each with a fixed time commitment.

0102030405
  1. 01

    2 hours

    The bottleneck diagnosis

    The workflow & conversion audit

    Before I touch any code, I find where the hours actually go. I audit your traffic, your manual data entry, and the stack you already run. You get back a list of the specific operations capping your scale.

    Bottleneck lattice

  2. 02

    1 day

    The visual architecture map

    See the system before it exists

    You should never pay for a black box. I map the whole system visually first. You see how a lead comes in, how it gets qualified, and where the data lands. All of it before one automation gets built.

    Schematic drawing itself

  3. 03

    1 week

    The engineering sprint

    Design & build, tailored to your brand

    Working from the architecture map, I build the system and design the screens around your brand, not a template. You see the design and sign off before anything gets wired up.

    Screens assembling

  4. 04

    3 days

    The load test

    The pressure test

    A system that breaks under traffic is a liability. I run it at volume before you do, with lead surges and the edge cases that break things. The bar is simple: nothing gets dropped.

    Structure under load

  5. 05

    2 hours

    The zero-hostage handover

    SOPs & full ownership

    Most agencies lock you into their code so you have to keep paying them. I do the opposite. You get the working system plus video SOPs showing your team how to run and change it. You own all of it.

    The vault, opened
