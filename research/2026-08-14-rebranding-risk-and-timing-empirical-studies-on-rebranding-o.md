  - Use the “evolutionary vs. transformational” scale‑of‑change lens to gauge execution risk, required internal change, and potential strategic payoff [6].

- Test strategic justification and organizational readiness
  - Confirm that a rebrand is anchored in real, long‑term business change and goals; avoid cosmetic rationale. Tie scope and timing to strategy rather than aesthetics [5][8].
  - Assess internal alignment and readiness; plan for employee sense‑making and stakeholder engagement to reduce resistance and execution risk [4].

- Anticipate stakeholder perception shifts and risks
  - Recognize that even modest changes affect stakeholder perceptions; explicitly evaluate necessity, timing, and likely customer/partner reactions before proceeding [2][8].

- Clarify external signaling objectives and limits
  - If signaling strategic repositioning or reputational distance is a goal, pressure‑test whether identity change alone is likely to achieve it; expect uncertainty in outcomes, as illustrated by Meta’s case [7].
