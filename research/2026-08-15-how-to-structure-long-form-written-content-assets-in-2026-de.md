## Answer

Long-form assets like definitive guides and pillar pages should run 1,200–5,000 words, opening with a hook, followed by structured sections, and closing with a clear call to action. Gating high-value content selectively is effective for lead generation, though it reduces reach. Successful examples include HubSpot's ebooks and LinkedIn's whitepapers.
