## Detail
Regulatory environment and scope  
- FBR issued Sales Tax General Order (STGO) No. 05 of 2026 (ICT), mandating POS integration and electronic invoicing for service providers operating in Islamabad Capital Territory. This directive is communicated in professional and advisory posts summarizing the requirement. [2][8]  
- Separately, FBR’s long-running policy requires Tier-1 retailers (including restaurants in that category) to integrate POS with FBR so that each transaction is reported in real time; integrated invoices carry an FBR QR code for verification. Practitioner and vendor guides in 2026 restate these obligations and outcomes. [3][5]

Enforcement posture in Islamabad  
- The Regional Tax Office (RTO) Islamabad has undertaken enforcement actions: a press statement reports two restaurants in F-7 and F-10 were sealed under the Sales Tax Act and POS Integration Rules after verification of non-compliance. [1]  
- FBR also documents sealing restaurants for issuing fake receipts/invoices, positioning these actions within a broader drive to bring Tier-1 Retailers/Restaurants under the POS invoicing system (noting association with the POS Prize Scheme in the enforcement narrative). [6]  
- Together, these show active 2026 enforcement in Islamabad targeting POS/e-invoicing compliance in the restaurant segment. [1][6]

What POS integration entails for restaurants  
- Guidance emphasizes that FBR POS integration connects every point-of-sale at each outlet to FBR’s system so all sales are reported electronically, and receipts include an FBR QR code. [3][5]  
- Vendor guidance aimed at restaurants highlights that integrated POS helps eliminate manual calculations and speeds checkout while aligning with federal tax compliance requirements. [4][5]

Market size and structure in ICT  
- As of April 1, 2026, ICT hosts 1,175 restaurants, a 5.67% increase from 2023. Of these, 1,119 (95.23%) are single-owner operations and 56 (4.77%) belong to larger brands. [7]
