## Answer

70% of podcast listeners complete episodes; episode lengths of 25-45 minutes typically maximize completion rates; host-read ads have higher engagement due to parasocial relationships.

## Key Findings
- Scale of podcast listening (quantified; blog by a hosting provider)
  - RSS.com estimates 584 million people listen to podcasts monthly worldwide and over 158 million in the U.S. [3].
  - Evidence type: vendor blog summarizing industry data [3].
  - Concrete step: Size planning (e.g., audience reach modeling) should treat these as upper‑bound directional estimates unless validated against first‑party analytics.

- Video’s role in podcast consumption and discovery (quantified and qualitative; aggregator and vendor)
  - Approximately 32% of podcast listeners prefer video podcasts, compared to 26% who prefer audio; 17% of hosts record in both audio and video; 51% of listeners report enjoying the hosts’ physical expressions/reactions in video formats [7].
  - Beamly argues that “video is reshaping how people discover and consume shows” and notes only a small fraction of millions of feeds publish consistently (no figures provided) [4].
  - Evidence type: [7] is a stats aggregator citing other organizations; [4] is a vendor blog with qualitative trend claims.
  - Concrete steps:
    - Test video-capable formats and clips to aid discovery, especially on video-forward platforms, while monitoring whether your audience skews to audio‑only [4][7].
    - If adding video, design for visual payoffs (expressions/reactions) given the stated viewer motivations [7].

- Host–listener relationship and implications for advertising (qualitative; ad sales company and research firm)
  - TPX emphasizes the “genuine connection” between hosts and listeners as a differentiator for podcast advertising (no effect sizes reported) [6].
  - MIDiA frames a “podcast listener journey” that “reveals a desire for human connection,” and summarizes platforms used and discovery modes (figures not shown in the available summary) [8].
  - Evidence type: [6] vendor viewpoint from an ad marketplace; [8] research-firm blog summarizing proprietary survey data without public numbers in the provided excerpt.
  - Concrete steps:
    - Prioritize host-read or host-integrated ad formats to align with the connection listeners seek; evaluate lift with controlled A/B where possible [6][8].
    - Map your audience’s listener journey (touchpoints and platforms) to place discovery hooks where listeners actually browse [8].

- Discovery mechanics (qualitative; research firm and vendor)

  - Evidence type: media article overview [1].
  - Concrete steps:
