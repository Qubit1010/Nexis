## Detail
Follower growth (personal B2B, 1,000–5,000 followers)
- No source in the provided set quantifies monthly follower growth for personal B2B profiles. The closest, quantifiable benchmark is for company pages: smaller pages (1,000–5,000 followers) average 40.75% annual growth and growth slows as pages get larger [2].  
- Converting 40.75% annual growth to a monthly rate yields approximately 2.88% per month ((1+0.4075)^(1/12)−1). Applied to starting follower counts:  
  - 1,000 followers: ~29 net-new followers/month  
  - 5,000 followers: ~144 net-new followers/month  
  These figures are page-based, not profile-based, and should be treated only as a directional proxy for personal B2B profiles [2].
