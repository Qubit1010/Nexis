## Answer

In 2026, quality backlinks from authoritative sources still matter, but tactics like PBN links and bulk directory submissions are penalized. Digital PR and genuine guest posts work best. Focus on earned links and brand mentions.

## Summary
In 2026, backlinks still matter, but off-page SEO has broadened to include digital PR, brand signals (including unlinked mentions), reviews, and citations. The strongest gains come from editorially earned coverage and mentions, while several manipulative link tactics were devalued following 2026 updates.

## Key Findings
1) Backlinks remain a primary off-page signal in 2026, with major guides still centering link acquisition as a core lever [1][5][7].  
2) Digital PR and editorially earned coverage continue to work, producing both links and brand mentions that build authority and trust [8][3][7][5].  
3) Brand signals and unlinked brand mentions are explicitly cited as important off-page signals in 2026, not just traditional backlinks [7][4][1].  
4) After March 2026, three tactics were notably devalued: sponsored guest posts on high-DA general news sites, niche edits on aged/thin-content domains, and PBN links (even with improved on-page content) [6].  
5) Outreach to journalists and resource page link building are promoted as viable, sustainable approaches in 2026 playbooks [3].  
6) Local citations, business directory listings, and reviews contribute to trust and visibility as part of an expanded off-page footprint [7][5].  
7) Link quality emphasis is on editorial links and broader trust/authority signals (E-E-A-T), not just raw link counts [8][1][7].  
8) Relative weighting appears to be shifting: links still matter, but brand signals and E-E-A-T are increasingly emphasized alongside them [1][4][7][5].  
9) The sources highlight what to stop doing broadly (“unsafe” tactics) and specify devalued link schemes, but offer limited guidance on toxic link cleanup/disavow processes [6][8].

## Detail
Scope of off-page SEO in 2026
- Authoritative guides frame off-page SEO as more than link building: it includes backlinks, brand signals, E-E-A-T, PR coverage, citations, and reviews that together indicate trust and authority [1][5][7].  
- Backlinko’s 2026 guide explicitly covers fundamentals beyond links, adding brand signals and E-E-A-T to the off-page toolkit [1]. Neel Networks likewise lists brand mentions, social signals, digital PR, directory listings, reviews, and data consistency across the web as key off-page elements, while still calling backlinks “the most important” off-page signal [7]. Ranking Studios echoes that the remit includes reviews, local citations, and community participation in addition to links [5].

What still works in 2026
- Editorial digital PR: Several sources underline digital PR and editorially earned placements as reliable ways to build authority, yielding both links and brand mentions [8][3][7][5].  
- Resource page link building and journalist outreach: Off-page playbooks continue to recommend targeted outreach strategies, including resource page link building and journalist outreach as sustainable methods, in contrast to paid placements on general news sites [3].  
- Brand mentions (linked and unlinked): Multiple sources highlight that unlinked brand citations and broader brand signals contribute to off-page authority in 2026, reflecting an evolution beyond pure hyperlink counts [7][4][1].  
- Citations, directories, and reviews: Local citations, directory listings, and review profiles remain part of the off-page authority mix, contributing to trust and visibility [7][5].  
- Community participation: Participating in relevant communities is specifically recommended as part of off-page activity in 2026 [5].

Tactics devalued or to stop
- Post-update devaluations: A March 2026 shift devalued three common tactics:  
  1) Sponsored guest posts on high-DA general news sites,  
  2) Niche edit placements on aged domains with thin content, and  
  3) PBN links, even when the content quality on those networks had been upgraded [6].  
  The same source notes rapid impact (“55% Sites Impacted in 2 Weeks”) and indicates a recovery horizon (“90 days Recovery Timeline”), alongside the strengthening of several surviving tactics [6].  
- “What to stop doing”: 2026 guidance stresses avoiding unsafe/manipulative off-page tactics and prioritizing trust-building methods (digital PR, editorial links, mentions) [8].  
- Guest posting nuance: While guest posting broadly is not condemned across sources, sponsored guest posts on general news sites are singled out as devalued in 2026 [6]. The reviewed sources do not make broader, categorical claims about all guest posting.

Link quality criteria in 2026
- Editorial nature of links: The emphasis is on links that are editorially given rather than placed via sponsorships or networks [8].  
- Authority, trust, and E-E-A-T signals: Off-page strategies increasingly tie link value to broader signals of expertise and trust. Backlinko’s 2026 guide explicitly positions E-E-A-T improvements within off-page strategy, indicating that off-page work should cultivate real-world authority and credibility [1]. Neel Networks notes that consistent, accurate business information, reviews, and PR coverage help search engines judge whether a site is a trusted, authoritative resource [7].  
- Not just volume: The 2026 guidance stresses quality and trust over raw link totals, aligning with the push toward editorial coverage and brand-building [8][1][7].

Digital PR, HARO, and journalist outreach
- The playbook sources promote digital PR and journalist outreach as ongoing pillars for earning coverage and links in 2026 [3][8].  
- Specific workflows or platforms (such as HARO) are not detailed in the provided sources; references remain at the strategy level (digital PR, journalist outreach, resource pages) [3][8].

Unlinked brand mentions
- Unlinked brand mentions are explicitly included in off-page signals for 2026 [7].  
- A 2026 discussion on the evolution of off-page signals argues that brand mentions are gaining relevance in the broader balance of signals relative to traditional backlinks [4].  
- Backlinko’s inclusion of “Generate Brand Signals” in an off-page guide reinforces that beyond hyperlinks, visible, credible brand presence is part of what search engines evaluate [1].

Toxic links and disavow
- The provided sources do not furnish detailed, prescriptive guidance on toxic link identification or disavow protocols. The clearest 2026 update insight is devaluation of certain link schemes, rather than instructions on cleanup or disavow [6]. No step-by-step disavow recommendations are provided in these materials [1][5][6][7][8].

How much backlinks still matter in 2026
- Links continue to be presented as the most important off-page signal in 2026 by Neel Networks, with other sources reaffirming that boosting off-page SEO with backlinks remains central [7][1]. Ranking Studios also asserts that backlinks remain a strong lever within a wider off-page mix [5].  
- However, emphasis is clearly expanding to brand signals and E-E-A-T: Backlinko includes brand signals and E-E-A-T as off-page focal points [1]; Search Atlas’s 2026 discussion frames a shift toward the value of brand mentions [4]; Neel Networks enumerates multiple non-link signals (reviews, citations, accuracy/consistency of business data) as part of trust-building [7].  
- Taken together, the sources suggest a blended model in 2026: backlinks are still critical, but brand signals, mentions, and demonstrable expertise and trust increasingly determine off-page success [1][4][7][5].

## Gaps / Caveats
- Quantitative weightings: None of the sources provide hard, comparable weighting metrics for backlinks versus brand signals/E-E-A-T; emphasis trends are directional rather than numeric [1][4][5][7].  
- Toxic links/disavow: There is little explicit guidance on diagnosing toxic links or executing disavow; post-update commentary focuses on devaluation of certain tactics rather than cleanup procedures [6].  
- HARO specificity: While journalist outreach and digital PR are recommended, the sources do not detail specific platforms or workflows (e.g., HARO) [3][8].  
- Guest posting boundaries: One source flags devaluation of sponsored guest posts on general news sites, but the broader boundaries of acceptable guest posting are not extensively defined across the provided materials [6].
