## Answer

Randomized field experiments show email personalization increases open rates by 20% and click-through rates, with no product-specific content. Studies by Sahni, Wheeler, and Chintagunta found a 31% increase in sales leads and a 17% reduction in unsubscribes. Apple's Mail Privacy Protection complicates open-rate measurement validity.
