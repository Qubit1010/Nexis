## Answer

Shaabiat Al Cartoon is an Emirati animated comedy series that revolves around social and cultural values in Emirati society, first broadcast in 2006 during Ramadan on Sama Dubai. It has been compared to The Simpsons and South Park, and it discusses various social issues faced by Arab families and individuals living in Dubai in a humorous way. The show has been cancelled as of Ramadan 2025. It is produced by Fanar Production and sponsored by Etisalat. The series has been successful year after year, providing zany yet clever takes on everyday UAE life and social issues.
