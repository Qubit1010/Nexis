## Answer

Qasr Al Hosn is a significant cultural heritage symbol in Abu Dhabi, serving as the ancestral home of the Al Nahyan Royal Family and a living memorial of the city's history. It has been home to the ruling family, the seat of government, a consultative council, and a national archive. The site also includes the National Consultative Council building and the Cultural Foundation. The Al Hosn Festival showcases Abu Dhabi’s heritage through performances, art installations, and traditional crafts. The Sheikh Zayed Palace Museum in Al Ain, part of the cultural scene, preserves the legacy of Sheikh Zayed Bin Sultan Al Nahyan. Falconry is deeply ingrained in Emirati culture and is celebrated during the festival. The sources do not provide specific information on the visual identity of the Sheikh Zayed legacy.
